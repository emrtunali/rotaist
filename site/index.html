How it works

  1. Real traffic. İBB publishes average speed for every ~1 km cell of Istanbul, every hour. Four months (Oct 2024 – Jan 2025) over Kadıköy, Üsküdar and Ataşehir become a day × hour × cell tensor.
  2. Street network. 33,604 OpenStreetMap street segments inherit the congestion of their cell, so a 50 km/h avenue crawls at 18:00 and flows at 06:00.
  3. Day-ahead forecast. A gradient-boosted model predicts tomorrow's congestion for every cell and hour using only data up to tonight.
  4. Time-dependent routing. Rotaist's own ALNS search plans vans on hour-by-hour travel times — it knows that the same 3 km takes twice as long in the evening rush and sequences stops around it.
  5. Honest back-test. Every plan is driven on the traffic that actually happened that day.

Orders are synthetic (drawn from real OSM residential floor area); traffic and streets are real. Data: İBB Açık Veri Portalı (İBB Open Data License) · © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) · tiles OpenFreeMap.
