How it works

🚦

Real traffic

İBB average speed for every ~1 km cell, every hour — 398 cells × 117 days.

→
🛣️

Street network

33,604 OpenStreetMap segments inherit their cell's congestion, hour by hour.

→
🔮

Day-ahead forecast

Gradient boosting predicts tomorrow's congestion from data up to tonight.

→
🧠

Time-dependent ALNS

Our optimiser knows 3 km takes twice as long at 18:00 and sequences around it.
